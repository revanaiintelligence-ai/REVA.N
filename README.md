#REVA.N
Reasoning Engine for Vision and Analysis Networking
REVA.N is an evolving architecture for structured reasoning, analysis, knowledge integration, and decision support.

Purpose
REVA.N is designed to connect observation, evidence, knowledge, analysis, reasoning, and evaluation within a structured framework.
Its objective is to help organize complex information, identify relationships, evaluate alternatives, and support better-informed decisions.
Core Areas
Structured reasoning
Vision and observation
Evidence and analysis
Knowledge integration
Decision evaluation
Systems and architecture
Human-centered decision support

SBDE
The Sistema de Extracción, Análisis, Auditoría y Aplicación de Sabiduría Bíblica (SBDE) is a research component associated with the REVA.N architecture.
It explores a controlled methodology for extracting principles from biblical texts, analyzing their context, and evaluating whether those principles can be transferred responsibly to other domains.

Development
REVA.N is an evolving project. Its architecture, methodology, documentation, and applications are continuously being developed and evaluated.
