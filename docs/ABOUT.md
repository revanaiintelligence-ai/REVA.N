# REVA.N — About

## What is REVA.N?

REVA.N (Reasoning Engine for Vision and Analysis Networking) is an evolving architecture for structured reasoning, analysis, knowledge integration, and decision support.

REVA.N is designed to connect observation, evidence, knowledge, reasoning, analysis, and evaluation within a structured framework.

## Purpose

The purpose of REVA.N is to help organize complex information, identify relationships, evaluate alternatives, and support better-informed decisions.

## Architecture

REVA.N is conceived as a modular architecture. Its components and methodologies are developed progressively and documented as the project evolves.

## SBDE

SBDE — Sistema de Extracción, Análisis, Auditoría y Aplicación de Sabiduría Bíblica — is a research component associated with the REVA.N architecture.

It establishes a controlled methodology for studying biblical texts, extracting principles from their context, and evaluating their responsible application to other domains.

## Development Status

REVA.N is an evolving research and development project.

The architecture, methodologies, documentation, and applications are continuously being developed, evaluated, and refined.
