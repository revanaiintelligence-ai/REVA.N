# REVA.N — Architecture

## Overview

REVA.N is conceived as a modular architecture for structured reasoning, analysis, knowledge integration, and decision support.

The architecture is designed to connect different forms of information and reasoning within a structured process rather than treating them as isolated elements.

## Core Flow

The conceptual flow of REVA.N is:

Observation → Evidence → Knowledge → Analysis → Reasoning → Evaluation → Decision

Each stage contributes to the construction and evaluation of a decision.

## Main Components

### Vision and Observation

The system begins with the observation of relevant information, situations, systems, or environments.

### Evidence

Observed information is examined against available evidence and reliable sources.

### Knowledge

Relevant knowledge is integrated to provide context and meaning.

### Analysis

Variables, relationships, patterns, risks, and opportunities are examined.

### Reasoning

The available information is connected through structured reasoning.

### Evaluation

Possible results, consequences, alternatives, and uncertainties are evaluated.

### Decision Support

The final objective is to provide structured information that can help a human make a better-informed decision.

## Modular Design

REVA.N is intended to evolve through independent but connected modules.

Modules may address different domains, methodologies, analytical processes, or applications while maintaining the underlying architecture.

## Human Role

REVA.N is designed as a decision-support architecture.

It does not replace human responsibility, judgment, or accountability.

## Development Status

The architecture is under continuous research and development.

Its components, methodologies, and applications may evolve as new evidence, testing, and research become available.
